## ✨ Features & Upgrades

### 1. Lightweight Interactive Hero Product Viewer
- **Positioning**: Split hero layout with editorial headline, designer metadata, live pricing, and CTA on the left, paired with the interactive multi-angle viewer on the right.
- **Multiple Authentic Angles**:
  - `3/4 Perspective`: Captures the embracing barrel curve and splayed leg geometry.
  - `Front Elevation`: Direct elevation highlighting symmetrical contouring and cushion depth.
  - `Side Profile`: Side silhouette showing the reclined recline posture and arm sweep.
  - `Rear Contour`: Continuous unbroken upholstery arc and rear timber cradle.
  - `Artisan Joinery`: Macro close-up of solid walnut, brushed brass, and bouclé weave.
- **Intuitive Controls**: Drag / swipe to rotate between available viewing angles, next/prev arrow buttons, keyboard arrow navigation, and direct angle tabs.
- **Detail Zoom Lens**: Magnifying inspect mode allowing users to examine fine fabric loops and timber grain.
- **Authentic Finish Switching**: Direct quick-switching between Cream Bouclé, Cognac Aniline Leather, Terracotta Velvet, and Oat Linen with authentic studio photography.

### 2. Verified Architectural Blueprint & Dimensions Section
- Architectural schematic diagram toggle between Front Elevation and Side Profile with dimension callout hairlines.
- Real-time unit switch between **Metric (mm / cm)** and **Imperial (in / lbs)**.
- Material construction breakdown detailing internal armature and timber joinery.

### 3. Spatial "Place in Your Room" Visualization Studio
- **Custom Upload**: Upload your own room photograph (drag & drop or file picker) or select from three curated interior backgrounds (Travertine Living Room, Walnut Reading Nook, Microcement Studio).
- **Proportional Scaling**: Real-time scale slider (0.55x to 1.55x) with calibrated 1:1 scale reference.
- **Natural Interaction**: Drag-to-position chair across the room with realistic contact ground shadow and floor alignment rotation slider (-25° to +25°).
- **Finish Consistency**: Synchronized upholstery finishes between the viewer, customizer, and room visualizer.

### 4. Favicon & Metadata
- Custom SVG favicon depicting the ARC's distinctive curved barrel shell and splayed legs.
- Verified title: `ARC Lounge Chair | Timeless Designer Comfort`.
- Polished OpenGraph social share card metadata.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Architecture

- **Framework**: React 19, TypeScript, Vite
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS, CSS Custom Properties (`--cream`, `--beige`, `--sand`, `--walnut`, `--espresso`, `--brass`, `--ink`)
- **Typography**: Cormorant Garamond (display serif) + DM Sans (UI sans)
- **Zero-3D Overhead**: Pure image-based sequence and lightweight DOM transformations for instant LCP (<1s), zero memory leaks, and 60 FPS mobile responsiveness.
